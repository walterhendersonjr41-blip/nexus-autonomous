        How the scores work

        • Proven done: actions marked done that have proof attached, out of all actions.
        • Trust score per agent (0–100): proven done = full credit, done without proof = half, partly done = quarter, failed or unclear = zero. Under 5 actions it says "too early to tell".
        • Hands-free: share of actions that didn't need you to step in.
        • Drift: the last 7 days compared with the 21 days before. Alerts if success drops 20+ points, you have to step in 20+ points more, cost per job doubles, or an agent goes quiet.
        • No number is ever invented. Empty means no data.
